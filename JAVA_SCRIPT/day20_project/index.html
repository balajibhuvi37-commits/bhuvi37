3-Year IT Timeline

How technology roles may evolve with AI.

2026 — AI Adoption

Companies are increasingly using AI tools for coding, testing, customer support and data analysis.

2027 — AI + Humans

Developers and other IT professionals will increasingly use AI as a productivity partner rather than working alone.

2028 — Intelligent IT

Routine tasks may become heavily automated while human skills such as architecture, problem solving and decision making become more valuable.

2029 — New IT Roles

The industry may create new roles around AI systems, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, data and AI-assisted software development.

What Happens To IT Jobs?

Some tasks may disappear. Some jobs may transform. New roles will also emerge.

💻

Software Developer

Developers may use AI to generate boilerplate code, debug errors and accelerate development.

🧪

Software Tester

AI can automate repetitive test cases, while humans focus on complex testing and product quality.

☁️

Cloud Engineer

Cloud infrastructure will continue to need people who understand deployment, security and architecture.

🔐

Cybersecurity

🤖

AI Engineer

AI engineers build, integrate and maintain intelligent systems used by modern companies.

📊

Data Engineer

Companies need reliable data pipelines and systems to power analytics and AI applications.
